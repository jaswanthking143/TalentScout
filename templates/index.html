TalentScout

Resume Intake & Role-Fit Analyzer

Intelligent Resume Intake & Skill Matching Platform

TalentScout simplifies the hiring workflow by automating candidate intake and skill verification. The application allows talent acquisition teams to upload candidate resumes, extract candidate technical skills, score role compatibility, and export structured recruitment reports.

TalentScout Architecture & Workflow

The system operates across three primary stages of the recruitment lifecycle.

1

Resume Parsing

Parses uploaded PDF resumes to extract name, contact details, experience, and key technical skills.

2

Role Scoring Engine

Compares candidate skill profiles against defined target roles (e.g. Python Full Stack) to compute percentage match scores.

3

Structured PDF Export

Generates actionable top-candidate candidate summary reports ready for download.
